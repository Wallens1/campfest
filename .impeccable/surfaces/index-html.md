# Inicio público de Camp Fest Bugalagrande

Mode: Persuade. Audience: young people (mostly on phones), parents and guardians of minors, and public and private allies. Job: understand the postponement to the first half of 2027, trust the project (legal basis, viability, budget), and sign up. Constraints: Memphis world, palette and animations stay as they are; icons replace emoji; no amounts in pesos; about 150 young people; registration open with no closing date; the API may be down, so the page must work without it.

## Direction contract

THESIS: This page is a public trust board. Every fact about the camp shows its status and its source. It refuses the category default of a festive hero followed by a pile of identical cards, and it refuses an "everything is ready" tone.

OWN-WORLD: Fredoka for display, Poppins for body text, thick #1A1A1E ink borders, solid offset shadows, and confetti and floating shapes on a #8B2FF0 hero over a #FFF7EA ground. Status stamps are rotated ink-bordered rectangles: cyan means confirmed, yellow means in progress, pink means postponed. Icons are a custom SVG set with a 2px stroke. Each section owns a whole band in one palette color.

STORY: The visitor learns the camp moves to the first half of 2027 and that its support was postponed but not withdrawn. Next they see the law that backs it (Ley 1622, Ley 1885), what makes it viable, and who funds each line item. Then they believe the project is serious and sign up, or, if they are an ally, keep their commitment.

FIRST VIEWPORT: On the left of the purple hero, the "Camp Fest Bugalagrande" wordmark in large uppercase Fredoka, the subtitle "II Campamento Juvenil del Valle del Cauca", a date badge reading "Primer semestre 2027", and the "Inscribirme" primary button. On the right, a white status-board card with an ink border and a 6px shadow, holding five rows (fecha, inscripciones, sede, aliados, cupos), each with an icon, a short value and a stamp. On mobile the board stacks below the actions.

FORM: Trust board (tablero de confianza), position 6 of 7 on the ordered list, dealt as the lead. Seed key 0cb895be.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
